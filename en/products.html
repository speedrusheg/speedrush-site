Tayyarak

How it works

From order to door in three steps

1

The customer orders

The customer picks a restaurant in their city, adds items and sends the order from the app.

2

The restaurant prepares

The order reaches the restaurant app instantly. The restaurant accepts it, prepares it and marks it ready.

3

The driver delivers

A driver picks up the order and delivers it, while the customer follows its status until it arrives.

Platform

Four parts, one system

Customer app

Browse restaurants and menus, place an order and follow it through to delivery.

Restaurant app

Receive and accept orders, set which items are available, and print orders.

Driver app

Take delivery jobs, get to the restaurant and then the customer, and confirm delivery.

Admin panel

Manage cities, restaurants, menus and drivers, follow orders and run the service.
